## The Six Drives™

### Drive 1, Hierarchy
**The Need:** To know I matter.

**Healthy:** Grounded. Clear. Worth is structural, not performed.

**Fractured:** Over-achieving as survival. RAS scans for rejection everywhere.

---

### Drive 2, Anticipation
**The Need:** To know what's coming.

**Healthy:** Internal compass navigates uncertainty from authority.

**Fractured:** Anxiety disguised as control. RAS locks into worst-case scenarios.

---

### Drive 3, Sovereignty
**The Need:** To own my choices.

**Healthy:** Author of your own story. RAS filters for choices and openings.

**Fractured:** Successful on the outside. Captive on the inside. RAS filters through someone else's lens.

---

### Drive 4, Resonance
**The Need:** To be seen and safe.

**Healthy:** Nervous system learns being seen is safe. Belonging replaces performance.

**Fractured:** Connected to everyone. Known by no one. RAS treats vulnerability as threat.

---

### Drive 5, Expansion
**The Need:** To keep becoming.

**Healthy:** Growth is intrinsic. RAS scans for what's next.

**Fractured:** Everything achieved. Nothing felt. RAS stops finding open doors.

---

### Drive 6, Imprint
**The Need:** For my life to mean something.

**Healthy:** Observer has direction. RAS filters for contribution and legacy.

**Fractured:** Everything achieved, nothing matters. RAS has no anchor. Reality loses coherence.
