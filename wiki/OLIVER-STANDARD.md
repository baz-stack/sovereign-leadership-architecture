## The Six Properties

The Oliver Standard™ is composite. All six properties are required. None are substitutable.

*Five of six is not Oliver. The architecture is composite. The standard is held only when all six are present, observable, and ratified.*

---

### Property One, Decision Integrity
Decisions made from internal authority, not external validation. The RAMS-weighted decision filter is installed and operating. Consequential decisions are no longer made from fear, performance pressure, or the need for external confirmation.

**Observable signal:** The leader can articulate the internal architecture behind any significant decision made in the engagement period.

---

### Property Two, Engineered Capacity
The leader holds expanded weight without the weight distorting them. The energy economy is specified. Allostatic burden is managed as a strategic variable. The capacity protocols are installed and operating.

**Observable signal:** The leader sustains performance across the engagement period without the characteristic erosion pattern that preceded the work.

---

### Property Three, Architectural Authority
Authority occupied, not performed. The leader is not managing the impression of authority, they are the authority. Team responses shift. The room changes when they enter it not because of status but because of presence.

**Observable signal:** Documented shift in team gravity, reported by the leader, confirmed by sponsor observation where applicable.

---

### Property Four, Team Gravity
The team is pulled toward the leader's standard rather than managed toward it. Force multiplication is operating. The leader is building the next layer of leadership capacity around them, not remaining the single point of failure.

**Observable signal:** The leader can identify specific individuals in their team whose growth trajectory has accelerated as a direct result of the leader's own architectural shift.

---

### Property Five, Sovereign Self-Possession
The leader's identity is internally authored. They no longer outsource the question of who they are to the market, to outcomes, or to other people's responses. This is the property that makes succession safe.

**Observable signal:** The leader demonstrates consistent identity coherence across private and public contexts, the same person in both fields.

---

### Property Six, Ratified Architecture
The work is documented. The Ratified Architecture Document exists and is held on file. It contains: RAMS-weighted decision filter, energy economics specification, capacity protocols, team gravity architecture, identity sovereignty position.

**Observable signal:** The document exists. It has been reviewed at close. It is held by the sponsor where applicable. It is verifiable at any future point.

## The Verification Protocol

At close of engagement, a property-level review is conducted:

1. Each of the six properties is reviewed against its specified observable indicators
2. Minimum verification window: the full engagement period
3. Cross-source confirmation where applicable, leader self-report, sponsor observation, documented behavioral data
4. The Architecture Document is reviewed and ratified
5. The Constitutional Clause is countersigned at close
6. The sponsor holds the file

This is institutional verification. Not an advisory claim.
