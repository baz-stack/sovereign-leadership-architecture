## The Core Principle

Most leaders try to change their results by changing their actions.

The Sovereign Operating System™ changes the identity that generates the actions.

Two states:

**The Survival State**, the identity built to achieve, prove, perform, and protect. It built everything you have. It is now the ceiling on everything you want.

**The Decided State**, the identity of the sovereign leader. Not aspirational. Declared as current fact and rehearsed until the nervous system accepts it as the new baseline.

The shift is not motivational. It is architectural.

Completed twice daily, morning and evening.

| Column | What It Does |
