## The Definition

Paradynamic Healing™ is the practitioner-level energy healing methodology that trains a healer to work across dimensional layers of a client's field, using frequency, vibration, and structured intervention to restore what the work calls "natural order."

The core premise, carried forward from the original teaching material: a person's pain, blockage, or dysfunction is not random. It sits in a specific dimensional layer of their field (physical, mental, emotional, karmic, or higher), it carries an identifiable frequency signature, and it can be shifted by a trained healer who knows how to work that layer directly.

> "In order to start really understanding [this work], you have to be aware of the hurts in [people] and also what they do. The frequencies have the power to change worlds." — source transcript, [286_20288624_2024-06-13 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EX0BR77ICzh45iLbdFxD-XVohb41maQJ/view)

This framework consolidates and rebrands the prior "Dimensional Mastery" course content in its entirety. **"Dimensional Mastery" and "Divine Intervention" are retired as names.** Every technique, module, and concept formerly taught under those names now lives here, under Paradynamic Healing™.

## What "Divine Intervention" Was, and Where It Lives Now

This is the one named concept Baz specifically flagged, so it is documented in full before anything else.

In the source transcripts, "Divine Intervention" was not a separate module or a stand-alone product. It was the name given to the *mechanism* at the center of the entire methodology — the moment the healer, mid-session, creates what the material calls a "singularity" in the client's field:

> "The definition of divine intervention, divine intervention and dimensional mastery is involves the healer creating a singularity... This split enables the removal of all that is not part of natural order." — [286_20288624_2024-06-13 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EX0BR77ICzh45iLbdFxD-XVohb41maQJ/view)

> "So what is dimensional mastery? It is a dimensional and ancient... technique which involves manipulation of energy throughout the body. It calls for the source to intervene... to remove [de-aligning] elements [and restore] alignment." — same session

So structurally: **Divine Intervention = the core technique.** Dimensional Mastery = the umbrella name for the full methodology that technique sits inside. They were never two different offers — one was the name of the whole house, the other was the name of the room where the actual healing event happens.

In Paradynamic Healing™, this technique is renamed **The Convergence Point** — the moment in a session where the healer deliberately collapses a client's fragmented field state into one coherent, "natural order" state. It retains the described function (the healer initiating a decisive shift that removes what the source material calls "de-aligning elements") without carrying forward either retired name. Any prior client-facing or internal material that still says "Divine Intervention" or "Dimensional Mastery" should be treated as superseded by this document.

Isolated other "divine" references in the source material (divine timing, divine justice, divine right, divine feminine/masculine, surrender to "Divine Mother and Father") were session-specific coaching language used with individual clients, not a defined technique or module — these are not carried forward as named IP here.

## The Architecture

The source material organizes into a numbered module sequence delivered over a training period the transcripts describe as roughly **12 weeks** (["Over the next 12 weeks I will go into 144 dimensional vibrations and teach you what they do and also how to use them in day to day life," 181_16199299_2024-04-18 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W3mkv1nNWiYkANusT-OZp8jNi84MJb1t/view)). Module numbering is inconsistent across sessions (the transcripts explicitly reference Module 3, Module 7, and "module 10," among others), so the structure below groups the material by teaching arc rather than inventing a clean module-by-module map that the source doesn't fully support.

### Stage 1 — Foundations: Quantum Framing and the Dimensional Map

The course opens by pairing quantum physics language with a spiritual framework, then teaches a working map of layers the practitioner will operate in:

> "The extraordinary journey of multidimensional mastery starts with learning... the foundations are quantum physics and spiritual frameworks." — [181_16199299_2024-04-18 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W3mkv1nNWiYkANusT-OZp8jNi84MJb1t/view)

The dimensional map taught in the sessions (paraphrased from the 197_16669340_2024-04-25 session):

| Layer | What It Governs (per source material) |
|---|---|
| Dimension 7 | Physical/light layer — vibrancy, vitality, wellness, emotional and mental patterning |
| Dimension 8 | Past-life and karmic memory — déjà vu, karmic clearing |
| Dimension 9 | Channeling layer — "angelics," spirit guides, past/present/future reading |
| Dimension 10 | Visions, premonition, intuitive knowing |

> **[RESEARCH PENDING] Physics anchor.** The transcripts describe this as a "dimensional" map running from 1 through 21+ layers, and invoke "quantum physics," "string theory," and "multiversal theory" as grounding. Physics does use the term "dimension" (e.g., string theory's proposed extra spatial dimensions), but those are mathematical constructs with no established connection to karmic memory, past lives, or spirit communication, and no physics authority credits them with those properties. This is metaphor language borrowed from real physics vocabulary, not an application of physics. The [Physics Anchor Library™](../research/physics-anchor-library.md) does not currently contain — and should not be made to contain — a citation that grounds "dimension" used this way. **Editorial flag for Baz:** decide whether to keep this as explicitly-labeled metaphor/spiritual framework language, or drop the "quantum physics" and "string theory" framing, since attaching real physicists' names or theories to karmic/afterlife claims is the kind of claim that will not survive scrutiny from a physics-literate audience.

### Stage 2 — Vortex Recognition and Working with Energetic Portals

Documented in the "Dimensional-Mastery-Vortex-Recognition.pptx" deck (Module 3) and in the 227_17652907_2024-05-09 transcript. Vortices are taught as "concentrated spiral[s] of energy emanating from the earth... gateways [that] allow the flow of energy between dimensions." The module trains a healer to:

1. **Sense** a vortex (described sound and visual "signs of presence")
2. **Interact with and amplify** a vortex (claimed practical range: table-sized up to "500 yards")
3. **Close** negative vortices (six taught closure techniques, including a named "Graveyard vortex" pattern)
4. **Manipulate location and polarity** (including claimed use of specific gemstones — canary yellow diamond, turquoise — as "carriers")
5. **Create** a new vortex (described using the term "scalar waves," said to "alter traditional physics equations")

> **[RESEARCH PENDING] Physics anchor — editorial flag, not a citation.** "Scalar waves" as used in the deck ("a profound aspect of energy that alters traditional physics equations") is not a term with a settled meaning in mainstream physics; it is frequently used in pseudoscience contexts to imply free-energy or non-local effects that don't have experimental support. There is no physics authority to responsibly cite here. Recommend Baz either cut this specific claim or clearly label it as non-scientific/energetic-metaphor language before this framework goes public — presenting it as adjacent to real physics ("alters physics equations") is the highest-risk line in the entire source library.

### Stage 3 — Frequency, Vibration, and "Natural Order"

The throughline of the entire methodology: pain, illness, and dysfunction are framed as energy that is "out of natural order," correctable by resonating at specific frequencies.

> "Frequency is a vibration. Vibration is a thought... Healing is a thought, a word, a seed, a distinction, a concept, an action or an energy." — [286_20288624_2024-06-13 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EX0BR77ICzh45iLbdFxD-XVohb41maQJ/view)

Specific numeric claims made in the transcripts:
- **144 Hz** — described as the frequency of "natural order" / "the divinity frequency," concentrated in "144 circles" and tied to a torus-shaped energy model
- **432 Hz** — mentioned as widely available online
- **430 Hz** — described in the transcript as suppressed/"banned by everybody in government and music"
- **524 Hz** — described as "the frequency for love"

> **[RESEARCH PENDING] Physics anchor — do not cite, flag instead.** Frequency and vibration are real physical concepts (a frequency is a measurable rate of oscillation in Hz), but the specific claims above — that 144 Hz is "the divinity frequency" underlying "natural order," that 430 Hz was suppressed by governments, that love and gratitude correspond to specific Hz values — have no basis in acoustics, physiology, or any peer-reviewed source, and the [Physics Anchor Library™](../research/physics-anchor-library.md) has nothing that legitimately supports them. This is the second highest-risk claim set in the material. **Recommend Baz reframe these explicitly as symbolic/ceremonial numbers used in the practice** (which is defensible as a stated belief system) **rather than as claims about what these frequencies physically do or historically happened to them** (which is not defensible and invites easy public fact-checking against the "banned by governments" claim in particular).

### Stage 4 — The Convergence Point (formerly "Divine Intervention")

The mechanism at the center of the work, described in full above. In practice, per the transcripts, the healer:

1. Works with the client to identify which dimensional layer holds the presenting issue
2. Uses frequency/resonance work (breath, tone, intention) to bring the field into alignment
3. Initiates the Convergence Point — the healer "creating a singularity, one solid dimension in essence," which "enables the removal of all that is not part of natural order"
4. Anticipates and works with client reactions, which the material describes as sometimes "quite violent" (emotional outburst, physical jerk, realization)

> "Manifestations, sometimes manifestations will explode out of the client like a buckshot. When you are healing yourself there has to be a reaction." — [286_20288624_2024-06-13 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EX0BR77ICzh45iLbdFxD-XVohb41maQJ/view)

> **[RESEARCH PENDING] Physics anchor.** "Creating a singularity" borrows a real physics/mathematics term (a gravitational singularity). Used here to describe a healer-initiated shift in a client's "field," it is metaphor, not an application of physics, and has no citable physics authority. Keep it labeled as practice/energetic language, not as physics.

**Practitioner note carried forward as-is:** the source material is explicit that "there are also no shortcuts" and that healing work can surface fear, anger, hurt, or violent emotion as "part of natural order" moving through the client — this is described as expected, not a sign of harm, in the original teaching. Anyone delivering this work should be trauma-informed and should have a clear plan for client safety when strong emotional or physical reactions surface.

### Stage 5 — Advanced Applications: Quantum Entanglement, Time, and Teleportation

Later sessions extend the framework into claims about manipulating time and physical distance:

> "Time travel, teleportation is possible through that knowledge... Quantum teleportation has three elements: one, understanding of quantum [entanglement]..." — [181_16199299_2024-04-18 session](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W3mkv1nNWiYkANusT-OZp8jNi84MJb1t/view)

> **[RESEARCH PENDING] Physics anchor — editorial flag, strong recommendation to cut.** Quantum entanglement is real and experimentally established — the [2022 Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Zeilinger) went to Aspect, Clauser, and Zeilinger specifically for entanglement experiments (see [Physics Anchor Library™, Section 5](../research/physics-anchor-library.md)). But quantum entanglement does not enable communication faster than light, does not transmit energy or matter, and has no established relationship to human "teleportation" or physically moving objects between locations. The physics term is real; the claim built on top of it ("physically move something from one place to another") is not something any physics authority would support, and directly contradicts settled experimental findings (the no-communication theorem). **Recommend Baz remove or heavily reframe this claim before Paradynamic Healing™ ships publicly** — of everything in the source material, this is the single claim most likely to draw direct, easy-to-verify public correction from a physics-literate critic, because it misuses a Nobel-winning, well-documented result to imply something that result explicitly does not support.
