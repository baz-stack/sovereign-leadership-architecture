## 1. What the institution is

The institution is a **trainer-of-trainers** architecture. It does not scale by adding more of Baz's hours. It scales by producing faculty who carry the method with fidelity — **one bloodline**, one standard, no dilution.

The model rests on three legs, in this order:

1. **Academic authority** — the method is grounded in real, cited mechanics. The science earns the right to be believed.
2. **Method** — RAMS™ and its subordinate frameworks, taught to a fixed anatomy, reproducible by certified faculty.
3. **Immersion** — the change is embodied, not informational. Faculty are trained in the room, under load, to the same standard they will hold in others.

One bloodline means: every faculty member and every authorized licensee teaches the same architecture, in the same order, held to the same certification gate. The method survives the founder because it was architected to.

---

## 2. The standard: applied science first, then the bridge

The institution's standard is **applied science first**.

- **First, the mechanics.** Real, cited, accuracy-checked science — neuroscience, physiology, and (once sourced) the relevant physics. This is what earns the authority. Nothing is taught as mechanism unless the mechanism is real and referenced.
- **Then, the philosophy of application.** A plain-language bridge that makes the science clear and teachable to people who do not know the science. This bridge is the innovation. It is what turns verified mechanics into something a leader can actually run.

The order is non-negotiable. Science earns the authority. The plain-language bridge is the innovation. If the science is not yet sourced, the bridge is held — it is not published as if the science were settled.

This is what separates the institution from the commodity market: the market sells the bridge with no mechanics underneath it. The institution sells verified mechanics **and** the bridge that makes them usable.

## 3. The frame for the science layer

The RAMS curriculum references specific scientific substrates — among them the Reticular Activating System and the physiology of stress and recovery on the neuroscience side, and the observer effect / measurement in quantum mechanics on the physics side.

**Position for this tier, stated plainly:**

- The **neuroscience and physiology** substrate (attention filtering, autonomic regulation, recovery cycles) is drawn from established literature and will be cited precisely before any faculty-facing or licensee-facing claim is finalized.
- The **quantum-mechanics** substrate is treated as **`[RESEARCH PENDING]`** in its entirety at this tier. The observer-effect framing used in the curriculum drafts is, at present, a **teaching analogy**, not a validated physical claim about human identity. It is **not** to be presented as settled physics. Whether — and precisely how — any physics result can legitimately underwrite the philosophy layer is exactly what `research/RESEARCH-PLAN.md` exists to determine, against real papers and named physicists, accuracy-checked.

No physics or quantum sentence in this institution is promoted from analogy to asserted fact until it clears that research pass with a real citation.

## 4. Certification and fidelity (frame)

- Faculty complete the full curriculum themselves, documented and witnessed, before they are authorized to teach any module.
- The module anatomy and pillar order are preserved across every faculty member and licensee.
- Certification is a gate, not a certificate mill: it certifies who the faculty member is still becoming, held to a live standard, and recertified on a fixed cadence.
- Every deployment is auditable against the master curriculum.
