## 3. Method (how to run it — later, on Baz's go)

1. For each item, find the **real** primary source or authoritative review. Record: authors, title, venue, year, and a resolvable identifier (DOI / stable URL).
2. Write, in one or two sentences, **what the source actually supports** — and, critically, what it does **not** support.
3. Mark each item CONFIRMED (with citation) or keep it `[RESEARCH PENDING]`. Never move an item to CONFIRMED without a real citation.
4. For every claim, state the boundary: the exact point where the curriculum's language must soften from "is" to "is analogous to."
5. Nothing from this pass reaches a public file. Public-facing science claims are a separate, later decision and remain out of scope here.

## 4. Guardrails (non-negotiable)
